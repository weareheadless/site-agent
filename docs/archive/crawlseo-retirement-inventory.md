# CrawlSEO research producer retirement inventory

## Scope and current tenant boundary

This inventory records the Python call paths and UI/API readers that must be considered before removing HelloAda's local SEO research and recommendation producers. It is a source-code audit, not evidence that tenant data has migrated. The registry census was independently verified from `/SOCIAL/site-agent-api/websites/tenants.json` on 2026-10-25: 13 tenants total; `atelier-harmonie` and `oceanicvibes` are the two website-present tenants still configured with CrawlSEO local authority. Both have `seo.article_research.enabled=true`; `oceanicvibes` also has `seo.site_report.enabled=true`.

Those settings make a broad code deletion unsafe today. CrawlSEO connector operation, recommendation synchronization, and a configuration flag do not prove the external provider has imported HelloAda's prior research, exact owner decisions, candidate/reservation state, or reports.

### Scheduling and authority guards

`core/jobs.py:register_builtin` keeps the local SEO, site-report, article-research, and outcome jobs for local configurations, subject to their enable flags. For website-present Payload tenants using CrawlSEO authority, the CrawlSEO-specific registration path retains `growth_pending` and maintenance jobs while omitting duplicate local discovery/research jobs. Runtime guards in `_seo_research_cycle`, `_seo_site_report_cycle`, `_article_research_cycle`, `_seo_outcomes_cycle`, `_seo_insight`, `_article`, `strategist.run`, `report.weekly_report`, and `run_growth_cycle` protect against stale/manual invocation. The direct strategist/report guards preserve historical readers and do not affect generic non-migrated tenant behavior. `run_pending_growth` retains exact candidate and execution/receipt reconciliation while quarantining legacy discovery. These branches are migration safeguards, not proof that the modules are globally dead.

The removable monthly block was a second `material["authority"] == "crawlseo"` check nested under `trigger == "monthly"` after an unconditional earlier CrawlSEO-authority return. It could never run. It was removed, and `test_monthly_crawlseo_material_completes_without_local_paid_research` verifies that CrawlSEO material completes without entering local paid research.

## Dependencies that are not CrawlSEO duplicates

- Preserve the native design intake dependency on `brain.page_strategy.native_homepage_request`.
- Preserve `brain.growth.prepare` and the Growth candidate, owner-decision, Payload approval, deployment, publication, and receipt paths. They execute reviewed changes rather than discover/research new opportunities.
- Preserve CrawlSEO evidence readers and compatibility analytics unless their public APIs are separately retired.
- Preserve saved weekly report drafts, strategist cards, `cards_block`, report history APIs, and owner-facing historical readers. New weekly synthesis and strategist recommendation generation are explicitly unsupported for frozen/CrawlSEO-authoritative tenants; they continue normally for non-migrated tenants.
- Retain historic memory, provider reservation reconciliation, uncertainty handling, owner decisions, and report readers until migration evidence proves that each record is imported or safely terminal.

## Safe deletion sequence

1. Freeze local research/recommendation producers and inspect the actual scheduler registry for every tenant; do not rely on configuration alone. Keep the freeze active through failed or interrupted activation.
2. Verify exact registered tenant/project identity and current CrawlSEO server binding/capabilities. Keep GA4/GSC source ingestion and read APIs distinct from research-authority selection.
3. Inventory saved Growth runs, exact candidate/review package hashes, owner decisions, approvals, Payload revisions, deployment/publication receipts, local article requests, reports, and insights. Preserve exact candidate/reservation terminal work; quarantine only discovery work that has no candidate or owner decision.
4. Reconcile every paid reservation to an imported receipt or known provider-terminal outcome. Unknown/uncertain outcomes and settled reservations with missing actual-cost evidence block deletion. Keep the dispatch hold active until separately authorized budget-policy import is implemented and verified; paid import is not implemented, so paid activation must remain held.
5. Activate CrawlSEO authority only after preflight, freeze, exact route proof, and provider capability checks. Keep runtime verification and durable activation receipts. Confirm the replacement tenant Memory after registry reload is used for receipts.
6. Migrate and verify **all** applicable tenants, including `atelier-harmonie` and `oceanicvibes`. Confirm current remote state, owner-review controls, exact existing candidates, and imported or terminal reservation outcomes. A current CrawlSEO connector and active binding do not satisfy this condition by themselves.
7. In a later change, remove tenant-specific local producer schedules and then producer implementations only after all tenant settings and scheduled work have migrated. Keep report/history/read APIs until consumers and retention requirements are separately retired. Remove defaults/flags last, after a repository-wide reference and config migration audit.

## Exit conditions for declaring producer retirement

- All registered tenants have exact successful CrawlSEO configuration and runtime verification, with no local-fallback producer path.
- Actual scheduler inventories show no duplicate local discovery or paid producers. Active jobs are drained; there are no unknown provider outcomes or unresolved reconciliation records.
- Every candidate with an owner decision or Payload revision remains available and reaches an explicitly recorded terminal state; no pending approval, review artifact, or publication receipt is discarded.
- Every provider reservation has an authoritative imported receipt or known terminal outcome with actual cost. An absent paid-budget import is a hold, not a completed migration.
- Tenant-facing evidence, decision, Growth, and history APIs have explicit replacements or are intentionally retired with confirmed consumers; the design intake path remains intact.
- Tests cover local tenants, CrawlSEO-authoritative tenants, exact candidate preservation, provider uncertainty, reload/replacement Memory behavior, scheduler inventory, and the no-fallback invariant.
